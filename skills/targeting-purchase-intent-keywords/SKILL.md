# targeting-purchase-intent-keywords

Grow e-commerce organic revenue by focusing SEO effort on keywords with explicit purchase intent, then closing the content and conversion gaps around them.

## When to use

- You are doing SEO for an e-commerce brand and want to prioritise work by revenue impact
- You want to automate keyword research and content gap analysis with an AI agent
- You are auditing internal linking and want to surface high-intent pages

## Steps

1. **Find purchase-intent keywords**
   - Seed candidates: product category names, "buy X", "best X", "X vs Y", "X for [use case]"
   - Filter by: commercial/transactional intent (Google's search intent classification), monthly search volume, and keyword difficulty
   - Tools: Ahrefs, Semrush, Moz, or a free alternative (Google Search Console, Keyword Surfer)
   - With an AI agent: prompt it to generate a list of intent-qualified variants, then check volume/KD via an API or MCP tool

2. **Audit content and internal links for each keyword**
   - Does a page already rank for this keyword? If yes → optimise on-page signals
   - If no page targets it → identify or create the best candidate page
   - Check internal links: does the site link to this page from related high-traffic pages?
   - Add internal links from category pages, blog posts, and comparison pages pointing to the target

3. **Build or optimise conversion pages**
   - Ensure the landing page has a clear CTA, social proof, and product/category content matching the keyword
   - Add schema markup (Product, Review, BreadcrumbList) for rich results
   - A/B test headline and CTA copy for the top 3–5 purchase-intent pages

4. **Hand the research to an AI agent**
   - Provide the agent with: site URL, target keyword list, access to a crawl export or sitemap
   - Ask it to: identify content gaps, draft internal link suggestions, flag conversion pages lacking CTAs
   - Review its output, prioritise by estimated revenue impact (search volume × conversion rate × AOV)

## Pitfalls

- Informational keywords (how-to, what-is) have low purchase intent — filter them out early
- Adding too many internal links from a single page dilutes link equity; cap at 3–5 per page
- Conversion page optimisation without keyword targeting first has limited organic uplift
