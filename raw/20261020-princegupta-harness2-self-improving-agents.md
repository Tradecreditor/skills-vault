Google just open sourced Harness², a harness that lets your AI agents upgrade themselves without any retraining 🤯

It proposes edits to your agent's prompts, tools, memory and skills, tests them on the same task, and keeps what worked. Then it saves the lessons from every task, so each round of improvement starts smarter than the last.

It's basically recursive self-improvement for your agent's setup, not the model itself, and one round boosted benchmark scores by up to 11.5 points with the exact same model.
