## 摘要

Tino Su（@tino.su）用中文整理 DeepLearning.AI／Andrew Ng 發表嘅「The AI Engineering Skills Map」。據帖文，呢份能力地圖來自超過 10,000 筆職缺描述、數十場與 AI 專家、用人主管同獵頭嘅結構化訪談，再加問卷同公開資料，目的係畀開發者排學習優先次序，同畀雇主知道該請咩能力。能力唔限於掛住「AI Engineer」職稱嘅人，所有開發者都會需要。地圖分四大頂層能力：Building & Deploying AI Applications、Software Engineering Fundamentals、Using Coding Agents、Shaping the Build。帖文由主帖加兩則作者回覆組成，並附原文連結。

## Key facts

- Source cited: DeepLearning.AI / Andrew Ng, "The AI Engineering Skills Map" (The Batch), https://www.deeplearning.ai/the-batch/the-ai-engineering-skills-map — link card title: "Andrew Ng and DeepLearning.AI Present the Essential Skills Every AI Engineer Needs in 2026". The article itself was not fetched; everything below is the Threads author's Chinese summary.
- Method (per the post): 10,000+ job descriptions, dozens of structured interviews with AI experts, hiring managers and recruiters, plus surveys and public data. Ng reportedly described the process as like doing clustering on a huge dataset of job listings and expert interviews.
- Goals: let developers set learning priorities; tell employers which skills to hire for. The skills are not limited to people titled "AI Engineer"; all developers will need them.
- Reason for a new map (per the post): since 2022 the way software is built has fundamentally changed.
- **1. Building & Deploying AI Applications**: the key difference from non-AI apps is unpredictable output. Understand building blocks such as LLMs, RAG and agentic workflows, and use statistical methods so the system can be governed predictably; the core is a disciplined loop of evals and error analysis.
- **2. Software Engineering Fundamentals**: understand software deeply enough to weigh cost, scalability, reliability, speed, security and privacy, and so decide better on tech stack, architecture, data storage and testing.
- **3. Using Coding Agents**: a mental model of what agents can and cannot do, plus context management, planning-versus-execution trade-offs and agent orchestration. The key is knowing when to step in and when to let go.
- **4. Shaping the Build**: as agents get better at delivering to a spec, engineers move toward deciding the spec themselves; effective engineering needs product sense and an understanding of business context and customer goals.
