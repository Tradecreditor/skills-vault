## 摘要

Charlie Hills（@charliehills）喺 Claude Code 入面，用完全相同嘅 4 個 motion graphics prompt 比較 Sonnet 5.5 同 Opus 5.5，一次生成、不做修正。結果 Sonnet 5.5 總成本 $4.95、用時 19.5 分鐘，Opus 5.5 則係 $8.44、34.7 分鐘，Sonnet 平咗 41%、快約一半。不過 Opus 仍然喺 Anthropic 官方表上 8 項 benchmark 中領先 7 項，主要只係贏 2 至 3 分。作者結論係日常工作用 Sonnet，困難、開放式任務先用 Opus。適合想用 AI 做短片動畫、又要控制成本嘅創作者同顧問。

## Key facts

- Test: same four motion-graphics prompts given to Sonnet 5.5 and Opus 5.5 in Claude Code; first try, no fixes, every clip is untouched output.
- The four prompts: (1) a 15-second showreel: go all out; (2) a short film answering one question: why do we dream?; (3) a bar chart that morphs into a line; (4) a card that grows into a full app window.
- Totals: $4.95 on Sonnet vs $8.44 on Opus; 19.5 minutes vs 34.7 minutes. Headline claim: "41% less, in about half the time".
- Scale reference from the author: a freelance motion designer charges $500 to $2,000 per animation (the going rate).
- Opus still leads Anthropic's own table on seven of eight benchmarks, mostly by 2 to 3 points.
- Author's recommendation: Sonnet for daily work, keep Opus for the hard, open-ended stuff.
- Slide 8 has the first two prompts; all four are in the pack. CTA: "Comment MOTION and I'll send you the 13 motion skills and every prompt." (the pack itself was not captured).
- Format: carousel with side-by-side videos; only the caption text was captured.

## 點解值得留意

- 同 `skills/model-tiering` 嘅「貴模型規劃、Sonnet 執行」原則直接對應，呢個係一個有成本同時間數字嘅實測 data point。

- 同自己製作短影音內容有關：可用 Sonnet 做日常動畫草稿，Opus 留畀難度高嘅鏡頭，成本同出片速度都更可控。
- 留意成本同時間係作者自己一次性測試，樣本細，用嚟參考而唔係結論。
