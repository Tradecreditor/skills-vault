## 摘要
Romi Patel 喺 Reel 列出五個人哋用 Jev（快而平嘅分類／路由模型）砌出嚟嘅應用：收件箱分流、留言審核、即時動態過濾、超快瀏覽器 agent、同路由。共通模式係先用 Jev 以低成本快速篩走大堆資料，只將值得處理嘅部分交畀 GPT 或 Claude 呢類大模型。內容只靠 caption，語音未轉錄，但 caption 已包含所有要點同數字。發佈日期係由「1w」推算，只屬約數。

## Key facts
- Inbox triage: 1,000 emails, seven checks at once (invoices, brand deals, scams, urgency) in ~6 seconds for 9 cents; a GPT-class model took five minutes and 62 cents for one check.
- Comment moderation: 1,000 YouTube comments sorted by sentiment, type and reply-worthiness in 5 seconds for 5 cents; also flags churn-risk members and best testimonials.
- Live feed filtering: Chrome extension tagging X posts as they load (breaking news / golden nugget / AI slop).
- Ultra-fast browser agents: Jev picks buttons and fields, a small model types; Google Flights Zurich to London in 7 seconds for under half a cent.
- Routing: support tickets, insurance claims, choosing which AI model handles a request; Jev returns the route plus a confidence, low confidence goes to a human.
- Pattern: Jev sorts the pile fast and cheap, only the good part reaches a bigger model.

## 點解值得留意
- 係「小模型先分流，大模型後處理」嘅具體成本對比，可用於 SEO/GEO audit 同 inbox 類工作流。
- 路由加信心值再轉人手嘅做法，可套落 agent 平台。
