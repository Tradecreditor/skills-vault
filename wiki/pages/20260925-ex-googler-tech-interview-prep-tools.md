## 摘要

前 Google 工程師 Jason Zhang（今年被裁，正在搵工）喺呢條 reel 列出佢準備技術面試時主要用緊嘅 5 個工具：Hello Interview、Excalidraw、LeetCode、Claude 同 Wispr Flow。佢強調唔使花巧工具，其中 2 個甚至唔係面試專用，只係令工作更簡單（例如用講代替打字）。核心建議係保持簡單，專注做練習題同搞清楚基礎。Caption 本身只係工具清單，「點樣用每一個」要留言 "tools" 先會 DM 俾你，所以實際用法細節冇公開。適合準備軟件工程／system design 面試嘅人參考，亦係一個典型「留言攞清單」嘅短片引流例子。

## Key facts

- Tools named in the caption (no usage details in the public text):
  - **Hello Interview** (hellointerview.com) — interview prep site "loved by 100,000+ software engineers and managers"; free tracks for System Design, Coding, Low-Level Design, ML System Design, Behavioral and AI Enabled Coding; paid premium tier.
  - **Excalidraw** (excalidraw.com) — free collaborative whiteboard for hand-drawn-style diagrams; used here for system design sketches. Drawings are saved in browser storage unless exported.
  - **LeetCode** — coding practice problems.
  - **Claude** (Anthropic) — general AI assistant; not interview-specific.
  - **Wispr Flow** (wisprflow.ai) — AI voice-to-text dictation that cleans up filler words and self-corrections, works in every app on Mac, Windows, iPhone and Android; claims 220 wpm vs 45 wpm typing ("4x faster"). The caption's "talking instead of typing" tool.
- Author's advice: "Keep things simple and focus on doing practice questions and understanding the fundamentals."
- CTA pattern: "comment 'tools' and I'll send the full list + how I use each one myself" — the visible comments are almost all the keyword "Tools" (comment-to-DM lead magnet).
- Critical comments: "With AI this is mostly irrelevant"; "Give us the deep underground stuff cmon this is basics".
- Not captured: the reel's spoken content (no transcript reader available in this session).

## 點解值得留意

- Wispr Flow（講嘢代替打字）可以直接用嚟對 Claude Code／coding agent 口述長 prompt，對 Josep 日常開發 Iron Log、English Overload 可能慳唔少打字時間；vault 已有 whisperX 可作本地開源替代比較。
- Excalidraw 適合同香港中小企客講解 AI 系統架構時即場畫圖，免費、唔使登入。
- 呢條片示範咗「留言關鍵字 → DM 完整清單」嘅引流 CTA：15 個可見留言差唔多全部係 "Tools"，但亦有人嫌內容太基本——做 AI 短片時，公開內容要夠料先唔會俾人覺得係 bait。
